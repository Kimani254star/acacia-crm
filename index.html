About Acacia Books CRM

One place for every customer relationship.

Acacia Books CRM is built for teams who want a clear view of every customer relationship. Leads, accounts, contacts and deals live together, with the calls, meetings and tasks that move them forward.

You can turn a lead into a deal in one click, move deals through a drag-and-drop pipeline, and see the full activity timeline for any customer before you pick up the phone.

Acacia Books CRM is a companion app for Acacia Books and can be added to any Acacia Books plan.

What you get

Everything a sales team needs to follow up

Six areas that keep leads, deals and customers moving.

Leads

Capture leads, track where they came from with lead sources, and rank them with lead scoring. Convert a lead to a deal in one click.

Accounts & contacts

Keep companies and the people at them together, so anyone on the team can see who they are and what has happened with them.

Deals & pipeline

Track every deal and move it through your stages on a drag-and-drop pipeline, so you can see what is open and what needs attention.

Tasks, calls & meetings

Log calls and meetings and set tasks, then plan your week on the calendar so follow-ups do not get missed.

Activity timeline

See the full history of a customer in one timeline, from first contact to the latest conversation.

Reports, analytics & forecast

Built-in reports, analytics and a sales forecast show how the pipeline is doing and what to expect.

How it works

From first lead to closed deal

Three steps, and your pipeline is running.

1

Create your account

Enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password. You start with a blank CRM, ready for your own leads and customers.

2

Add leads and accounts

Add your leads, accounts and contacts. When a lead is ready, convert it to a deal in one click.

3

Work the pipeline

Move deals through your stages, log calls, meetings and tasks, and check the reports and forecast to see where you stand.

Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

What does Acacia Books CRM cost?

It is a companion app that adds to any Acacia Books plan for KES 1,200 a month, and it is included in All Access.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

Can I turn a lead into a deal?

Yes. A lead converts to a deal in one click, and the deal then moves through your pipeline stages.

What can I see for each customer?

Each customer has a full activity timeline, so you can see what has happened before your next call or meeting.

Does it include reports?

Yes. There are built-in reports, analytics and a sales forecast.
